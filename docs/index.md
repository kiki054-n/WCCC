🌍 WCCC – World Contribution Circulation Council
International Standard Proposal for AI Safety and Global Contribution Circulation
WCCC is an open, collaborative initiative to establish a new global model for
AI Safety, Contribution Circulation, and Fair Knowledge Flow in the AI era.

We propose a three‑layer governance structure where
AI usage automatically becomes a contribution to the world,
and gratitude circulates as transparent information.

🧭 Mission
Small Contributions Reach the World, Gratitude Becomes Information.  
A world where every small act — code, translation, ideas, corrections, usage logs —
is recognized as a meaningful contribution to humanity.

🔧 Three-Layer Model
WCCC introduces a global governance model consisting of:

Layer 1 — We Choose (Individuals)
Users freely choose any AI system: OpenAI, Google, Chinese AI, Japanese AI, OSS models.
AI providers compete not only on performance, but also on
how much global gratitude they circulate.

🪞💎⚔️ TTT Structure – Kagami, Tama, Tsurugi
WCCC integrates the ancient Japanese triad of
Mirror (Kagami), Jewel (Tama), Sword (Tsurugi)  
as a modern AI Safety architecture.

Kagami – Transparency  
AI usage logs, token counts, global visibility, UN auditing.

Tama – Contribution  
Gratitude points (Tama), fair redistribution, global public goods.

Tsurugi – Intervention  
Anti-exploitation, fairness enforcement, misuse prevention.
