# 🌍 Summary of the Proposal

The WCCC framework consists of three core components:

### **1. Three-Layer Model**  
A governance structure where:

- Individuals freely choose AI systems  
- National governments collect AI Contribution Tax (Tama Tax)  
- UN-WCCC circulates contributions globally to creators and public goods  

### **2. TTT Structure (Kagami, Tama, Tsurugi)**  
A cultural-philosophical foundation for AI safety:

- **Kagami – Transparency**  
- **Tama – Contribution**  
- **Tsurugi – Anti-Exploitation Intervention**

### **3. BFOL – Back-Flow Open License**  
A new open license ensuring:

- Free commercial use  
- Mandatory back-flow of benefits  
- Transparent usage metrics  
- Protection against exploitation  

Together, these components form a **non-hegemonic, globally fair AI governance model**  
that can be adopted by UN agencies and member states.
