# 🪞💎⚔️ TTT Structure – Kagami, Tama, Tsurugi
### Traditional Japanese Triad Reimagined for Global AI Safety

The **TTT Structure** is a modern reinterpretation of the ancient Japanese triad  
**Kagami (Mirror), Tama (Jewel), Tsurugi (Sword)**.  
WCCC applies this cultural-philosophical framework to build a  
**non-hegemonic, transparent, and fair AI Safety model** for the world.

TTT provides the conceptual backbone for WCCC’s governance architecture.

---

# 🪞 Kagami – The Mirror  
### *Transparency → Recognition → AI Safety*

**Kagami** represents *clarity, reflection, and truth*.  
In WCCC, Kagami is implemented as:

- Transparent AI usage logs  
- Token-count reporting without content inspection  
- Global visibility of contribution flows  
- UN-WCCC auditing for fairness and neutrality  
- Public dashboards showing global AI contribution circulation  

Kagami ensures that **AI systems cannot hide exploitation, misuse, or unfairness**.  
It is the foundation of **AI Safety** in the WCCC model.


---

# 💎 Tama – The Jewel  
### *Contribution → Circulation → Global Fairness*

**Tama** symbolizes *value, gratitude, and accumulation*.  
In WCCC, Tama becomes:

- **Gratitude Points (Tama)** for creators and contributors  
- Automatic redistribution of AI Contribution Tax (Tama Tax)  
- Funding for global public goods (education, climate, digital access)  
- Recognition of small contributions (code, translation, ideas, corrections)  
- A new happiness model for the AI era  

Tama ensures that **every small act of creation reaches the world**,  
and that **AI usage becomes a global contribution**.


---

# ⚔️ Tsurugi – The Sword  
### *Intervention → Protection → Anti-Exploitation*

**Tsurugi** represents *action, justice, and protection*.  
In WCCC, Tsurugi is implemented as:

- Anti-exploitation enforcement  
- Protection against hegemonic AI control  
- Prevention of unfair data extraction  
- Intervention in cases of misuse or unethical deployment  
- UN-WCCC authority to ensure global fairness  

Tsurugi ensures that **no nation, corporation, or AI system can dominate or exploit others**.


---

# 🌐 Integration: TTT as the Core of WCCC

TTT is not symbolic—it is a **functional governance architecture**.


Together, they form a **non-hegemonic, transparent, and fair AI governance model**  
that can be adopted by:

- UN agencies  
- OECD  
- UNESCO  
- ITU  
- Global AI governance bodies  
- Open-source communities  
- National governments
