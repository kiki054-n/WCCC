# 🌍 WCCC – UN Policy Proposal  
### International Standard for AI Safety, Contribution Circulation, and Fair Knowledge Flow  
### Submitted to: UN Global Digital Compact / UNESCO / ITU / OECD AI Governance

---

# 1. Executive Summary

Artificial Intelligence is reshaping global power structures, economic flows,  
and knowledge creation. Current governance models are fragmented,  
hegemonic, and insufficient to protect creators, ensure fairness,  
or prevent exploitation at global scale.

We propose the establishment of:

**WCCC – World Contribution Circulation Council**,  
a UN-aligned international framework that ensures:

- Transparent AI usage (Kagami – Mirror)  
- Fair global contribution circulation (Tama – Jewel)  
- Anti-exploitation enforcement (Tsurugi – Sword)  
- Non-hegemonic AI safety standards  
- Global redistribution of AI-generated value  

This proposal introduces the **Three-Layer Model**,  
the **TTT Structure**, and the **BFOL License**,  
forming a unified governance architecture for the AI era.

---

# 2. Background & Problem Statement

AI systems today rely on:

- Massive datasets created by millions of individuals  
- Open-source code contributed by global communities  
- Cultural, linguistic, and creative works from diverse societies  

Yet the benefits flow disproportionately to a small number of corporations  
and nations, creating:

- Global inequality  
- Knowledge extraction without compensation  
- Lack of transparency  
- Exploitation of creators  
- Absence of international safety standards  
- Growing geopolitical tensions around AI governance  

The world needs a **non-hegemonic, transparent, and fair AI governance model**  
that respects national sovereignty while ensuring global equity.

---

# 3. Proposed Solution: WCCC Framework

WCCC provides a unified governance architecture consisting of:

## 3.1 Three-Layer Model

### **Layer 1 – We Choose (Individuals)**  
Users freely choose any AI system.  
AI providers compete on performance and contribution circulation.

### **Layer 2 – We Collect (National Governments)**  
Countries collect AI Contribution Tax (Tama Tax)  
based on usage metrics (token counts).  
Content is never inspected.

### **Layer 3 – We Circulate (UN-WCCC)**  
A portion of national collections is remitted to the UN-WCCC Global Fund  
for redistribution to creators, communities, and public goods.

This model respects sovereignty, protects privacy,  
and ensures global fairness.

# 4. TTT Structure – Cultural Foundation for AI Safety

WCCC integrates the ancient Japanese triad:

### 🪞 **Kagami – Transparency**  
AI usage logs, token counts, UN auditing.

### 💎 **Tama – Contribution**  
Gratitude points, redistribution, public goods funding.

### ⚔️ **Tsurugi – Intervention**  
Anti-exploitation enforcement, fairness protection.

This provides a **non-military, non-hegemonic safety architecture**  
for global AI governance.

# 5. BFOL – Back-Flow Open License

BFOL is a new open license for the AI era.

It ensures:

- Free commercial use  
- Mandatory back-flow of benefits  
- Transparent usage metrics  
- Redistribution to creators and global public goods  
- Protection against exploitation  

BFOL can be adopted by:

- AI companies  
- Open-source communities  
- Governments  
- International organizations
