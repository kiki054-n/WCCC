# 🔧 Three-Layer Model – Global Architecture of WCCC

The Three-Layer Model is the core governance structure of  
**WCCC – World Contribution Circulation Council**,  
designed to establish an international standard for **AI Safety**,  
**Contribution Circulation**, and **Fair Knowledge Flow**.

This model ensures that **AI usage automatically becomes a contribution to the world**,  
while respecting national sovereignty, individual freedom, and global fairness.

---

## 🟦 Layer 1 — We Choose (Individuals)

Individuals freely choose any AI system:

- OpenAI  
- Google  
- Chinese AI  
- Japanese AI  
- OSS / community-driven models  

AI providers compete not only on performance, but also on  
**how much global gratitude they circulate**.


This layer preserves **market competition**, **user freedom**,  
and **non-hegemonic AI development**.

---

## 🟩 Layer 2 — We Collect (National Governments)

Each country collects **AI Contribution Tax (Tama Tax)**  
based on AI usage (token counts).

- National sovereignty is fully respected  
- No content is inspected (privacy protection)  
- Only usage metrics are reported  



This layer ensures **fair contribution collection**  
without surveillance or central control.

---

## 🟧 Layer 3 — We Circulate (UN-WCCC)

A portion of each nation’s collected Tama Tax is remitted to  
the **UN-WCCC Global Fund**, which manages global circulation.

Functions of UN-WCCC:

- Transparent auditing (Kagami)  
- Global contribution circulation (Tama)  
- Anti-exploitation intervention (Tsurugi)  
- Redistribution to creators, communities, and public goods  


This layer provides **global fairness**,  
**non-hegemonic AI governance**,  
and **international safety standards**.

---

## 🪞💎⚔️ Integration with TTT Structure

WCCC incorporates the ancient Japanese triad:

### **🪞 Kagami – Mirror (Transparency)**  
AI usage logs, token counts, global visibility, UN auditing.

### **💎 Tama – Jewel (Contribution)**  
Gratitude points, redistribution, public goods funding.

### **⚔️ Tsurugi – Sword (Intervention)**  
Anti-exploitation, fairness enforcement, misuse prevention.



## 🌐 Summary

The Three-Layer Model provides:

- A **non-hegemonic global AI safety framework**  
- A **transparent contribution circulation system**  
- A **UN-managed international governance structure**  
- A **new happiness model for the AI era**  

This architecture is designed to be adopted by  
**UN agencies, OECD, UNESCO, ITU, and global AI governance bodies**.

WCCC is an open, collaborative project.  
Everyone is invited to co-create the future of AI safety.
