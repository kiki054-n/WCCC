# 🚀 WCCC Roadmap – Global AI Safety & Contribution Circulation
### Development Plan for the World Contribution Circulation Council  
### Toward an International Standard for AI Safety and Fair Knowledge Flow

This roadmap outlines the development phases of **WCCC – World Contribution Circulation Council**,  
a global initiative to establish a **non-hegemonic AI safety model**,  
a **transparent contribution circulation system**,  
and a **UN-governed international standard** for the AI era.

---

# 🌍 Vision

A world where:

- AI is safe, fair, and non-exploitative  
- Small contributions reach the world  
- Gratitude becomes information  
- Knowledge circulates without domination  
- Every person’s existence has value  

WCCC is a new civilization model for the AI era.
